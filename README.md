# Global Measles (MCV1) Vaccination Project
In this project, I tested how global measles (MCV1) vaccination coverage changed over time, which countries are falling behind the WHO’s herd-immunity target, and what role did COVID – 19 and income level play in the recent declines.

Data source: WHO Immunization Coverage Estimates, accessed via Kaggle (MCV1.csv), covering 195 countries from 2000 – 2021. Income classification data from the World Bank (via Our World in Data).

Benchmark used throughout: WHO’S 95% coverage target, the threshold needed for herd immunity against measles, which is one of the most contagious diseases known, requiring very high population immunity to stop outbreaks.
